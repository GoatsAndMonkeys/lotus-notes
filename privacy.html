Privacy Policy

Music Lotus for iOS (TestFlight beta). Last updated 23 October 2026.

Music Lotus collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not sell or share anything about you.

What the app connects to

  • archive.org. The app fetches public concert recordings and their details from the Internet Archive. Like any website, archive.org receives your device's IP address and the requests it makes; its own terms and privacy policy apply.
  • This site. The app downloads public liner notes from goatsandmonkeys.github.io (GitHub Pages). It sends nothing but the request itself; GitHub's privacy statement covers its server logs.
  • Your own Lotus server (optional). If you enter the address of a Lotus server you run yourself, the app talks to that server directly. That data stays between you and your server; the developer never sees it.

What stays on your device

Favourite stations, "I was there" marks and settings are stored on your device only. They are not uploaded anywhere, and deleting the app deletes them. If you connect your own Lotus server, marks you save there are kept on that server, which you control.

TestFlight

As a TestFlight beta, Apple may share crash reports, usage data and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send with the developer, under Apple's privacy policy and TestFlight terms. The developer uses these only to fix the app.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and its date changed. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.

Music Lotus is a fan-made project, not affiliated with the Mountain Goats.
