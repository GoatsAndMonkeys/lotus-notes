Music Lotus

Live-show radio and liner notes for bands that allow taping of shows

Music Lotus is an iPhone app, now in TestFlight beta, that plays live recordings of bands that allow taping of their shows, from the Internet Archive's Live Music Archive. Pick a station (every recorded show, an era, or the rarities) and it plays; open any show for its setlist, the recommended recording, who taped it, and short liner notes that cite their sources. It is a fan-made project and is not affiliated with any of the bands or their labels.

Credits

The recordings are the work of the tapers and transferers who recorded these shows and shared them on archive.org, and of the Internet Archive, which hosts them. Every show in the app names its taper and links to their other recordings. Mountain Goats song annotations link to The Annotated Mountain Goats.

Liner notes

This site also hosts the app's liner notes: setlists, recording details and cited notes as signed, static JSON. Start at index.json; the signing key is .well-known/lotus-notes-key.json (key id 8S5sc7cJpSpOBVnivFR9tA). The notes hold public facts, short quotes and links: no audio, no images and no listener data.
